# Pitch

## One line
Aliens invaded. Bullets don't work. Crashing them at speed does, and they can't refuse a race.

## Why they race (pick or combine)
1. **Speed is their culture.** A warrior species whose only honor is the race. A challenge can't be refused, and dying in a crash is the only death they accept. You call them out one by one instead of fighting an army.
2. **Their tech only breaks at speed.** Shields are invulnerable at rest but destabilize past a threshold velocity. You have to get one flat out, then wreck it. Ties story directly to the takedown mechanic and to boost as a weapon.
3. **They feed on adrenaline.** They came for the rush; racing humans is their drug. The hardest bosses are the most addicted.

Leading option: 1 + 2 (speed-worshipping culture with speed-gated shields).

## Tone
Play it straight-faced, like *Mars Attacks* meets *Burnout*. The more seriously the world treats the absurd premise, the better it lands.

## Structure (draft)
- The city is the arena; districts fall to the invasion and are taken back one at a time.
- Progression through takedown counts and alien "crews" / bosses per district.
- "Conquer it all" arc: wreck your way through every district to the mothership-level finale.
