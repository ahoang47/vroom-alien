# Cars and handling (arcade)

Give each car one standout strength and one clear weakness so the choice matters.

## Racing levers
- **Top speed vs acceleration**: pulls hard off the line vs needs room to stretch.
- **Turn rate and grip**: sticky carver vs loose drifter.
- **Drift behavior**: ease of entry, drift line tightness, whether drifting keeps or bleeds speed.
- **Boost**: charge source (drift, slipstream, near miss, takedown), duration, kick size; chain small boosts vs one big burst.
- **Handling at speed**: twitchy at top speed vs planted the faster it goes.
- **Recovery**: how fast it gets back to speed after a hit or a wall.
- **Signature quirk**: one special trait per car (wider drift angle, extra boost slot, ramming without speed loss).

## Road Rage levers
- **Weight class**: heavies win shunts and shrug off hits but turn slowly; lights are fast and nimble but die to one good hit. Rock-paper-scissors of car types.
- **Hit strength**: damage dealt vs taken; better at side-swipes, rear-ends or head-ons.
- **Boost as a weapon**: earned by aggressive driving, so recklessness fuels you; cars earn it differently.
- **Durability**: hits before wrecking, and how fast you get back into the fight.
- **Takedown style**: pinning into walls, flipping, or shunting into traffic.

## Feel
Camera height, shake and FOV at speed, engine note, rumble and tire squeal sell the differences as much as the numbers.

## Risk
Combat can drown out driving. Keep a clean racing line rewarding so the best players mix speed and aggression.
